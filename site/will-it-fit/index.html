How it works

A room visualizer alone isn't enough. We check the move path.

1

Mark your doorway

Tap the edges to drop draggable handles. The app measures width, height, and detects the hinge side.

2

Set hinge and swing direction

Choose left or right hinge, swing in or out. The app calculates usable clearance at the hinge point.

3

Outline your item

Or enter dimensions manually. Toggle packaging to account for box size. Advanced options: remove door (+2–3 cm), rotate in corridor, allow tilt.

4

Get your result

Pass (green), Tight (amber with tips), or No-go (red with alternatives). See recommended approach: "Tilt 18° and rotate 45° through right hinge."

5

Share or export

Copy link, generate QR code, or export as PDF with measurements and confidence notes.

Frequently asked questions

Will‑It‑Fit works on iOS 12+, Android 7+, and modern desktop browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). AR features require iOS 11+ (ARKit) or Android 8+ (ARCore). Desktop users can scan a QR code to access AR on their phones.

No app required on most devices. Will‑It‑Fit runs entirely in your web browser. AR features use your device's built-in AR capabilities (Quick Look on iOS, Scene Viewer on Android).

Our homography-based 2D measurement system is accurate to within ±2 cm (±0.8 in) under good lighting with clear doorway edges. We provide confidence scores and allow manual dimension entry for critical measurements.

Our tool provides recommendations when items are tight or won't fit: remove the door for extra clearance, tilt and rotate at specific angles, remove feet/packaging, or consider partial disassembly. We calculate the exact margin so you know if it's close.

The free consumer version supports unlimited doorway checks for one space. Pro and Business plans support multiple projects, spaces, and full move path analysis from front door to final room.

Yes. We process images on-device whenever possible. When cloud processing is needed, all data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and images are deleted after analysis. We never sell or share your personal data.

We offer native plugins for Shopify, BigCommerce, and Adobe Commerce, plus a JavaScript SDK and REST API for custom integrations. Our team provides implementation support and developer documentation. Contact us for a demo.

Room fit checks if an item fits in your space (floor area, wall clearance). Doorway fit is the critical step most AR tools miss: ensuring the item can actually pass through your doorway considering hinge placement, swing direction, and corner clearance. We check both.

Absolutely. Our Pro and Business plans are designed for moving and delivery teams to pre-qualify jobs, reduce failed deliveries, and provide accurate quotes. Features include multi-doorway analysis, stairway checks, and PDF reports.

Free users have access to our help center and community forum. Pro users get email support. Business customers receive priority support with guaranteed response times and a dedicated account manager.
